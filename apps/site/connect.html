KanTV on your iPhone, with your real TV

An iPhone's browser can't talk to a TV by itself, so a small program on your computer does it. Run it, scan its QR code, and KanTV opens in Safari connected to your TV.

  • A Windows PC or a Mac
  • An iPhone
  • A Google TV or Android TV
  • All three on the same Wi-Fi

  1. Download KanTV Connect

    One file, nothing to install.

    Download

  2. Open it

    Double-click kantv-connect-windows.exe.

    If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”: choose More info, then Run anyway. When it asks about network access, tick Private networks and choose Allow.

    Open the downloaded zip, then double-click kantv-connect. A Terminal window opens; that's it running.

    If macOS says it can't be opened: open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and choose Open Anyway, then open it again. If it asks to accept incoming network connections, choose Allow.

    Unzip it and run ./kantv-connect in a terminal.

    A page with a QR code opens on the computer. Keep the program running while you use KanTV.

  3. Scan the QR code with your iPhone

    Open the Camera, point it at the code on the computer's screen and tap the link. KanTV opens in Safari. You can add it to your Home Screen from the Share menu.

  4. Tap “Set up my TV”

    KanTV walks you through switching on developer options on the TV. When the TV asks “Allow USB debugging?”, tick Always allow from this computer and choose Allow.

    Nothing is uninstalled. Restore my TV in the app puts everything back, any time, for free.

If something doesn't work

The QR code link doesn't open on the iPhone

The iPhone must be on the same Wi-Fi as the computer, not on mobile data or a guest network. You can also type the link shown under the QR code into Safari, including the part after ?key=.

KanTV can't find the TV

Tap “My TV isn't showing up” and type the TV's IP address (on the TV: Settings → Network & Internet → your network). Some routers keep devices apart on guest or “isolated” networks; use the main Wi-Fi.

“This TV needs a pairing code”

Some newer TVs only offer Wireless debugging with a pairing code. This early version connects to TVs with USB debugging or Network debugging; pairing-code support is coming.

“The TV didn't answer”

Check that USB debugging (or Network debugging) is on in the TV's Developer options, that the TV is awake, and that KanTV Connect is still running on the computer. Then tap Try again.

Is it safe?

KanTV Connect only listens on your home network, and only a phone that opened its link (with its key) can use it. The TV asks you to allow it once. When setup finishes, KanTV switches debugging off again, so nothing can reach the TV that way afterwards.
