Set up KanTV in three steps

  1. 1. Wireless debugging on the TV

    The app shows the menus on your TV, step by step, and switches debugging off again at the end. No cable.

  2. 2. The tested list for your model

    KanTV loads the list tested on your model. Untick anything you use, such as casting from your phone.

  3. 3. KanTV cleans up the TV

    It saves a restore point, turns the apps off and checks the TV took every change.

Questions

What people ask before they set up a TV.

Which TVs does KanTV work on?

Google TV and Android TV: Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer, Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Xiaomi and Nvidia Shield. Samsung, LG, Roku and Vizio run other systems, so it can't set them up.

Does KanTV uninstall any apps?

No. Apps are turned off, not removed, and safety checks keep your inputs, Settings and Wi-Fi working.

Can KanTV undo its changes?

Yes. Restore turns every app back on and puts the stock home screen back. It is free on every plan.
